# Walkthrough: a new lodge secretary's first day

Run 6 Nov 2026 in the e2e app (PostgreSQL `club_manager_e2e`, queue worker, Mailpit), in the built-in browser.
Persona: a busy, non-technical secretary who has been told "our lodge is going to use ClubManager". Only menus, buttons and help text were used to find things; source code was read only afterwards, to explain two failures.

**Setup notes (test harness, not app findings)**
- `http://secretary.localhost:8001` does not work: the HTML loads, but the JS bundle comes from `APP_URL` (127.0.0.1:8001) and is blocked by CORS, so the page is blank. Two other walkthrough sessions were signed in on 127.0.0.1 in the same browser and kept replacing each other's login. I ran my own PHP server on `secretary.localhost:8002` (same e2e database, `APP_URL` set to that host), which gave me my own cookies. How to do it is in the shared notes folder (`wt-browser-isolation.md`).
- Native `confirm()` dialogs (`wire:confirm`) are closed automatically by the pane, so "Import members" looked dead until I accepted the dialog from a script. A real user sees "Import these rows into the member roster?". This is not an app bug.
- Data created: user `wt-sec.secretary@e2e.test` (registered at `/register`, email confirmed through Mailpit), lodge **"wt-sec Aarheus Lodge"** (club 23, slug `aarheus-lodge`). It was made by claiming directory lodge *Aarheus Lodge No. 6854* and approving the claim as `super@e2e.test`, which is how a real lodge is onboarded. Also 5 roster members `wt-sec.*@e2e.test`, member Arthur's account (invite accepted), meeting 99 (published), two payment options, one logo upload. Passwords are local test values and are kept out of this file. The directory lodge 6854 is now linked to club 23 in the e2e database.
- **One data fix outside the UI:** the club's `permission_matrix` was written once with tinker so that settings could be saved at all (see F1). Nothing else was changed outside the screens.

Rough effort for a real person: about 45 to 60 minutes, plus waiting for a platform admin to approve the claim. Without someone to explain F1 and F2, the day ends with settings that will not save and a summons nobody received.

## What went well
- The member import wizard (step 7): help on the page, a template, automatic column matching, UK date choice, review, undo and an import history.
- Payment options page: one card per way of paying, each with a plain explanation, "Not set up" / "In use" badges and a switch. Bank form help is clear ("Card and bank payments can only be discounted…").
- The Accounting "Get your accounting set up" checklist with To do badges. This is the only guided setup in the product.
- Claim form and superadmin claim queue: clear wording, "only ask to manage a lodge you belong to", one-click approve that creates the club and makes the claimant its owner.
- After sign-in you are taken back to the page you were trying to open. Success messages after saves ("Club settings updated successfully", "Financial year end updated").
- Choosing a Masonic hall fills in its address and pre-fills the meeting venue.
- The publish confirmation explains what will happen, and the invite welcome page shows the logo and motto.

## Top 10 friction points (ranked)

1. **F1 (High): a new lodge cannot save Club settings.** *Screen:* Club settings, any tab → Save changes. *What happened:* the page reloads and nothing is saved, with no message anywhere. The server answers `permission_matrix.manage_meetings.roles.N is invalid`. The default matrix in `config/club_permissions.php` gives `manage_meetings` the role `secretary`, and `UpdateClubSettingsRequest` only allows `owner, admin, coach, treasurer, member, examiner`. The value is hidden: the grid's "Secretary" column is `coach`, so ticking boxes cannot remove it. This hits every lodge that has never saved a permission matrix. *Change:* allow `secretary` in the rule, or drop it from the defaults (and map it to `coach`). Also show validation errors for fields on other tabs (a banner at the top, plus a mark on the tab).
2. **F2 (High): "Publish and send summons" emails only people who already have a portal account.** *Screen:* Secretary meeting dashboard. *What happened:* "emailed to 1 members" (the secretary). The 5 imported brethren (4 with email, 2 invited) got nothing. Publishing again sends no new emails, so members who accept their invite later are never emailed this summons. "Awaiting RSVP: 2" contradicts "1 members". *Change:* send to every active roster member who has an email (the passwordless RSVP link already works without an account). The confirm dialog should say "This emails N members; M have no email address", and there should be a "Send to members who have not had it" action.
3. **F3 (High): the summons preview shows made-up content as if it were real.** *Screen:* Summons PDF. *What happened:* "Officers for 2026-2027" lists sample names (W. Bro. K. D. Lord, Bro. A. Smith…), not the imported officers (WM Arthur, Treasurer David…). The cover calls the secretary "WBro Wendy Secretary, MASTER". "Tel: 07718 903347" was never entered. The rehearsal line says "6:00pm on Friday at the Masonic Hall" (the form says 17:30, and the meeting is a Thursday). The roll lists 3 of 6 people, all "WBro", all "2026". The cover says "PROVINCIAL GRAND LODGE" with no province name. *Change:* build these sections only from lodge data, leave them blank or hide them when empty, never fall back to samples, and flag empty sections before publishing.
4. **F4 (Medium): two different financial-year settings.** *Screens:* Club settings → Accounting ("Financial year start month", default January, which is stored but not used by reports) and Accounting → Settings ("Financial year end", default April, which is the one reports use). Setting October in the first leaves the second at April, and the checklist still says To do. *Change:* remove the Club settings field, or make it read and write `financial_year_end_month`, with one line saying "your year runs Oct–Sep".
5. **F5 (Medium): no way in for a new lodge.** *Screens:* home page (a developer demo hub: "PostgreSQL powered", rowing and rugby copy, The Lodge of Fraternity labelled "Rowing"), Register ("member account"), and the dashboard (which lists only hosted clubs). Claiming a lodge is found only through My lodges → "Find more lodges". *Change:* add "Run your lodge here? Find it and claim it" to the home page, the register page and the empty dashboard, linking to `/lodges`. Show the pending claim on the dashboard with an expected wait.
6. **F6 (Medium): bank details have to be entered in three places.** *Screens:* Payment options (events), Club settings → Accounting ("sort code / account number (summons dues and dining)"), and each summons' Festive board tab. The meeting form does not use what was set up in Payment options, and its placeholders look like real numbers (20-82-18 / 80288373). *Change:* keep one set of lodge bank details, pre-fill the festive board from it, make the payment options apply to meeting dining as well, and use obviously fake placeholders.
7. **F7 (Medium): no first-day checklist in admin.** *Screen:* admin Dashboard shows tiles that are all zero. *Change:* reuse the Accounting checklist pattern on the admin dashboard: lodge details, logo, members, invite, payment options, first meeting, publish. Each item links to its screen and ticks off once done.
8. **F8 (Medium): rowing and demo defaults leak into a Masonic lodge.** New lodge defaults: member portal "Senior Rower Tier £35.00 a month", "Upcoming events and training", tagline "Excellence in Club Management", invoice footer "Fees support equipment & clubhouse operations", registration mode "Anyone can join", member number #MEM-7459 although the prefix is AARH-. Placeholders mention "OUBC-", "Excellence on the Isis & Thames", and another lodge ("The Lodge of Fraternity will be holding their Annual Burns Night"). *Change:* seed defaults by club type, start new lodges as "Invite only", and remove tenant names from placeholders.
9. **F9 (Low-Med): Club settings pages read like developer screens.** The "Details" summary shows example placeholders as if they were data ("Consecration date 3rd April 1873", "Meeting formula 4th Thu. 1 To 11 Ex. 6, 7, 8") while the fields are empty. "Subscription payment provider" mentions "Stripe Elements & Cashier subscription webhooks", "Merchant of Record" and "Sandbox demo mode". There are two save buttons ("Save changes" and "Save all settings"), and the left menu keeps "General" highlighted on other tabs. *Change:* show "Not set" for empty values, write the provider text in plain English (or hide it until the lodge takes dues online), and use one save button.
10. **F10 (Low): smaller things a secretary trips over.** Meeting cards show "at 18:30" with no date. The logo preview and admin sidebar keep the initials. The sort code is not checked (5 digits accepted). The CSV "Title" column (W Bro / Bro) is not matched to Masonic rank automatically. The invitation's sender name is the old lodge name ("Aarheus Lodge") while the subject uses the new one, and it greets "Dear Arthur Wtsec-Allen" without rank or saying who invited him. The secretary is not on her own roster, so she is not in the officer list. The claim form's text boxes and tick-box have no labels (screen readers hear "on"). The lodge page for a London lodge links to a Worcestershire library catalogue and shows an empty card. The approval email links to the member portal rather than admin. The summons email has no dress, agenda, dining price or reply-by date, and no PDF.

## What a secretary would need explained
- That a lodge is set up by **finding it in "Find a lodge" and claiming it**, and that a person checks the claim. Until then the account looks like a plain member account.
- That **admin is behind the "Admin" button** (top right of the member portal), and that the member portal and admin are two different places.
- That the **roster** (people on the lodge's books) and **portal accounts** (people who have accepted an invite) are different things, and that today only portal accounts receive summons emails (F2).
- Which **financial year** setting counts: Accounting → Settings → "Financial year end" (F4).
- That "Payment options" cover **events**, while meeting dining has its own bank fields on each summons (F6).
- That the summons PDF can contain **sample officers and contact details**: check the "Officers for the year" and "Front page" tabs before publishing (F3).
- That "Publish and send summons" is on the meeting's **Secretary dashboard**, not on the Edit summons screen, and that publishing again does not email anyone new.
- What "Secretary" means in Roles and permissions (it is the internal `coach` role), and that new lodges start with "Anyone can join".

## Not covered
Card or PayPal setup (needs Stripe/PayPal keys), the member-side RSVP click-through, a phone-sized screen, and "Generate season / Masonic year". The claim was approved by me acting as superadmin, so the real wait for approval was not measured.
