# Look and feel test: phone and tablet widths, dark mode, empty and large data, keyboard (2 Nov 2026)

Tested against the e2e app (PostgreSQL `club_manager_e2e`, frontend as built). Test only: no application code was changed, nothing committed.

**Method.** Every screen below was loaded in a hidden iframe at 320, 375, 640, 768 and 1280 px wide (so media queries fire at that width) from a logged-in page, in light and dark mode, and measured: page scroll width against viewport width, the first element that crosses the edge (ignoring anything inside its own scroll container), text that touches the edge, tap target sizes, unlabelled inputs and icon buttons, computed text and background colours (WCAG ratio, alpha-blended up the tree; gradients and images skipped), `undefined`/`null`/`NaN`/`{{` text, `<title>`, `<h1>` count and `<main>`. About 140 URLs x 3 widths in light, 100 of them again in dark, 34 at 640 and 320. Key screens were also opened by hand in my own browser tabs (screenshots, keyboard). A read-only code audit by a subagent (Vue, Blade, CSS) backs the accessibility items marked "code". The machine was at load average 44 and the shared dev server is single threaded, so I ran my own copy of the e2e server (port 8002, same database, 6 workers) and stopped it at the end.

**Test data.** Throwaway lodge `ui-e2e` (club id 5, admin `ui-admin@e2e.test`, `member.a3` and `member.a4` as members) plus 321 `ui-` roster members (one with a 60 character surname and a 100 character email), a £1,234,567.89 + £987,654.32 collection, one event and one newsletter. All of it is deleted (club, accounts, pages, tags, channels, contacts, roster, event, bookings, newsletter and deliveries, the `ui-admin` user and its sessions). Mailpit was not touched. The newsletter test sent 3 emails to `@e2e.test` addresses.

## Findings (not fixed)

**F160 Medium.** Tables with a hidden "Actions" or similar header make the whole page scroll sideways. A `span.sr-only` (position absolute) inside a table that sits in an `overflow-x-auto` box which is not `position: relative` escapes the box, so the page's scroll width becomes the table's width. Seen on `/{club}/admin/members` (375: 1039 px, 768 and 1280: 1311 px, so even a desktop shows a horizontal scrollbar; with the 321-row roster 1244 px at 375 and 1516 px at 1280), `/admin/members/import` (479 at 375, 825 at 768), `/admin/meetings/{id}` (587 / 859), `/admin/meetings/{id}/dining` (502 / 774), `/admin/newsletters/subscribers` (906 / 1194), `/admin/accounting/journal-entries/create` (658 / 930). Fix direction: put `relative` on every scroll wrapper around a table (or on the shared table component) so the clip applies; check with `document.documentElement.scrollWidth`.

**F161 Medium.** `/members/directory` at 375 and 320 is 647 px wide: the subscription rows inside each lodge card (channel name and description beside a "Subscribe" button) do not wrap, so the whole page, header included, is wider than the screen and the phone shows it zoomed out. Make the row wrap or stack below `sm`.

**F162 Medium.** Public lodge directory header (`/lodges`, `/lodges?...`, `/lodges/{slug}`, `/lodges/halls/{slug}`) at 375 and 320: the bar (logo, "Find a lodge", dark mode switch, "Sign in/My ...") needs 414 px, so the right button is cut off, "Find a lodge" breaks into three lines, and the page scrolls sideways. Also `/lodges` page 2 and similar: a card for a lodge with a long unbroken name (the `<script>alert(1)</script>Sec Evil & Co Lodge` row from the security tests, escaped correctly) widens the list to 518 px; add `min-w-0 break-words` on card text.

**F163 Medium.** Public club website (`/site/lodge-of-fraternity` and each page, 375/768/1280): (a) the nav has no mobile menu, all pages are shown as wrapped pills and take about 230 px of the first screen at 375 (with 40 pages in the nav that is most of the screen); (b) one page title with 2,000 px of unbroken text (`bbbb...`, from the website-builder tests) makes the whole site scroll sideways at every width (scroll width 2293 px). Needs a collapsing menu below `md` and `truncate`/`max-w` on nav items. The logo link also has no accessible name.

**F164 Medium.** Admin dashboard (`/{club}/admin/analytics`, the "Dashboard" menu item) shows made-up content: `Pages/Admin/Analytics.vue` hard-codes "+100.00" and "+20.00" under the top cards, falls back to `300` active users and `£2,500` sales when the real figure is 0 (`metrics.total_members || 300`, `metrics.monthly_dues_est || '2,500'`), and a fixed schedule "Club members meeting 08:00 to 10:00", "Cheer & dance", "Conditioning" with placeholder bars. A brand-new lodge sees it (verified on the empty lodge: "TOTAL SALES £360.00", the three fake sessions under "Today"). Replace with real data or empty states.

**F165 Low.** `/{club}/admin/charity/festival` on a lodge with no festival set: "Bronze tier £0 Reached", "Silver tier £0 Reached", "Honour tier: Platinum Honor", "Relief Chest ref: ·" with nothing after the dot, "0% complete". Show an empty state ("No festival set up yet") until a target exists.

**F166 Medium.** Two `<h1>` on about 35 screens: members pages have an sr-only layout `h1` plus the page's visible `h1` (`/members/dashboard`, calendar, events, meetings, news, dues, notifications, directory, lodges, signed documents, security, `/members/{slug}/meetings`, news, calendar), and admin pages get the `AdminLayout` `h1` plus `PageHeader` (`/admin/users/{id}`, settings, visitors, officers, accounting bills/invoices/contacts/journal create, bank accounts, bank imports, reconciliation workspace, dues-subscriptions, subscriptions, committee, minutes, candidates, charity x3, media manager). Zero `<h1>` on `/admin/media` and `/admin/pages/places` is because those two URLs are JSON endpoints for the pickers (not pages). Heading level jumps h1 to h3 on `/admin/accounting/reporting/profit_and_loss`, `/admin/accounting`, `/admin/bank-imports`, `/admin/candidates`, `/admin/charity` (code: `Components/Ui/PageHeader.vue:24`, `Layouts/AdminLayout.vue:342`, `MembersLayout.vue:156`).

**F167 Medium.** Landmarks and skip link. No skip-to-content link and 12 tab stops before the content in `AdminLayout` (verified at `/ui-e2e/admin/events`), `SuperAdminLayout`, `PublicLayout` (`/lodges`) and the Livewire layout; the members layout and club site have a working one. `<nav>` has no `aria-label` and nothing has `aria-current`, so the current section is shown by colour only. No `<main>` at all on `/login`, `/register`, `/forgot-password`, `/lodges/{slug}/claim`, `/lodges/{slug}/suggest-fix`, `/directory`, `/{club}/visitor-register`, `/site/{club}/news/{post}`, `/summons/rsvp/...`, `/sign/...`, and the print pages `/admin/events/{id}/guest-list` and `/catering` (these also have no `h1`; the guest list table is 386 px wide at 375). Nothing announces route changes to screen readers (code).

**F168 Medium.** Icon-only buttons without a name: the mobile admin menu button (`button.p-2.rounded-xl`, `AdminLayout.vue:86`, Livewire `components/layouts/app.blade.php:46`; on every admin page at 375 and no `aria-expanded`), 6 more on `/admin/bank-reconciliation`, 12 more on `/admin/pages/create` and `/admin/pages/{id}/edit` (`button.rounded-xl.border` icon buttons in the editor), the logo link on the public club site. Add `aria-label` and `aria-expanded`.

**F169 Medium.** Colour contrast under AA (4.5:1, large text 3:1), light mode, computed from rendered colours: grey secondary text `text-slate-400` (#908b84) on white is 3.38:1 (3.24:1 on the tinted panels) on at least 30 screens, for example "No billing receipts found" (`/members/{slug}/dues`), "Sent 2 Nov 2026" and "-" (admin members, meetings), "Not set" (accounting settings), "Navigation pages" (every website builder page), "« Previous" (all paged lists), "Meeting times not listed" (`/lodges`). Success green text (0,153,102) is 3.65:1 on white: white label on the green "Update password" button (`/members/profile`, `/admin/profile`), report amounts (budget vs actual "£460.42"), "Present" on committee minutes (3.54:1). Helper text under the new-event "They don't need an account..." is 2.42:1 (`/admin/events/create`, `/edit`), "Emails a private link so..." 2.33:1 (`/admin/members/create`), the "Move" block button in the post editor 2.27:1 (light) and 2.12:1 (dark). Dark mode has only a handful, all between 3.4 and 4.5:1 ("+4 more" in the calendar, "None known"). Disabled primary buttons (2.9:1) were not counted.

**F170 Medium.** Form controls without a label: the three password fields on `/members/profile` and `/admin/profile` (5 inputs), the subject input and body textarea on `/{club}/admin/email-templates` and `/superadmin/email-templates`, 5 inputs in the booking form on `/site/{club}/events/{slug}`; code audit: 26 `<label>`s without `for` in `Public/Event.vue`, `Admin/Profile/Edit.vue`, `ReconcilePanel.vue`, `EmailTemplateEditor.vue`, `PageHeaderFooterPane.vue`, and placeholder-only inputs in `Lodges/Show.vue:143`.

**F171 Low.** Tap targets under 40 px: standard buttons and links 30 to 32 px tall (every admin and members action button, "Back to ..." links, tab strip links at 32 px), filter and tab pills 26 px (`/members/events`, `/members/dues`, `/members/notifications`), checkboxes and "Remember me" 16 px, media manager selection boxes 16 x 16, up/down arrows in payment options 36 x 30, "CM" logo link 32 x 32, dialog close button about 28 px, "Read article" link 83 x 16. `/admin/pages/overview` has 525 targets under 36 px. Raise the default button height on `< sm`, or add padding.

**F172 Low to medium.** `prefers-reduced-motion` is ignored: no `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` or `motion-reduce:` anywhere (0 hits in the built CSS, 2 JS checks in the counter and slideshow blocks); 12 `animate-*` uses and 2 smooth scrolls (`Admin/PageList.vue:743`, `Admin/PaymentOptions/Index.vue:72`).

**F173 Low.** Screen reader gaps (code plus checked in browser): field errors are tied to the field but not `role="alert"` (`Ui/Input.vue:62`, `Select.vue:63`, `Textarea.vue:60`; verified on the login error), `Alert.vue` is always `role="status"` even for danger; `AttachmentPreviewModal.vue` has no dialog role, Escape or focus trap; the club switcher, account menu and admin menus have no Escape; click-only `div`s with no keyboard route (`Admin/Media/Index.vue:1240`, `Member/Dashboard.vue:202`, `Club/Show.vue:322`); the Vue modal does not lock page scroll; three avatar `<img>` can lack `alt` (`AdminLayout.vue:367`).

**F174 Low.** Long unbroken text and filter rows overflow at phone widths: `/members/news` and `/members/{slug}/news` (a title such as `sec-io-xss-1 <script>...` pushes the card 14 px past 375: scroll width 389), `/members/{slug}/events` filter row "All (20) Meetings (4) ..." (389 at 375, 371 at 320; no wrap or scroll), `/members/{slug}/calendar` nav strip fine. Add `break-words` and `flex-wrap`/scroll to those rows.

**F175 Low.** After the session expires, saving a form sends the visitor to the login page (good) but with no "your session expired" message, and the unsaved text of a post is lost. Consider keeping the draft (the event form already has `useDraft`) or showing a message.

**Notes (not defects).** (1) Page titles end "- Laravel" in this env because `APP_NAME` is unset. (2) Around 22:03 and 22:04 (server clock) another test session flipped `member.a3`'s membership to `past` in lodge-of-fraternity and in my lodge and later restored it (role in my lodge changed to member), which broke my own pages for a few minutes; I used a separate admin for my lodge afterwards. (3) The browser pane shares cookies per hostname across sessions, so I used `look.localhost`/`guest.localhost`; two other sessions' logins on `localhost` had logged my browser out earlier.

## Not run

Payment double-click (a dues or event payment on throwaway data); a visible spinner or disabled state under a slow network (no throttling in this pane); server-side validation errors on the long admin forms (event, settings), where the browser's "required" check fires first; the bank import preview modal and reconciliation workspace interactions beyond load, overflow and contrast; website builder drag and drop, block editors at phone width and its Escape/focus behaviour; the committee live minute taker; screen reader speech (VoiceOver/NVDA); real touch devices and Safari/Firefox; print and PDF output; "near me" lodge search; tablet portrait editing flows in the builder; text-only zoom (browser font size) as opposed to viewport narrowing; colour contrast for images and charts (none found) and for the website themes other than the lodge's own.
