If a payment fails, the Billing page tells you, and the team has the free plan's features until the bill is paid. Nothing is deleted.

## What you will see

The **Billing** page of the team says that a payment has failed. Owners and admins can open it. Your data, members and settings stay where they are. Only the paid features are paused.

## How to fix it

1. Open the team and go to **Billing**.
2. Press **Card, address and VAT number**. Stripe's secure portal opens.
3. Update your card, or add a new one. See [Update your card](/help/update-your-card).
4. Save and return to {{product}}.

Once the payment goes through, your plan's features come back.

## Common reasons

- The card has expired or been replaced.
- There was not enough money on the card.
- Your bank blocked the payment and wants you to approve it.

## If something goes wrong

- If the new card is refused, ask your bank, or try another card.
- If you do not want to keep the plan, you can [cancel it](/help/change-or-cancel-your-plan) or choose a different one.
- If you cannot open the **Billing** page, ask an owner or admin of the team to fix it. See [What each role can do](/help/what-each-role-can-do).
- If the page still shows a problem after you have paid, refresh it after a few minutes.
