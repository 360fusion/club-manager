If you forgot your password, ask for a reset link from the sign-in page and choose a new one.

1. Go to [Sign in](/login) and press **Forgot your password?**. You can also go straight to [Forgot password](/forgot-password).
2. Type the email address you signed up with and send the request.
3. Open the email we send you and press the link in it.
4. On the **Choose a new password** page, type your new password and confirm it.
5. Sign in with the new password.

For your safety, the site shows the same answer whether or not the address has an account. So you will not see an error if you mistype the address. You will just not get an email.

If you know your current password and only want a new one, you do not need the reset email. See [Change your email or password](/help/change-your-email-or-password).

## If something goes wrong

- Check your spam folder, and check that you used the address you registered with. You may have more than one.
- Use the newest email. Older links can stop working once you ask again.
- If you signed up with Google or Microsoft, you may never have set a password. Use the **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** button on the sign-in page. See [Sign in with Google or Microsoft](/help/sign-in-with-google-or-microsoft).
- If you have two-factor sign-in on and lost your phone, use **Lost your phone? Use a recovery code** at sign-in.
