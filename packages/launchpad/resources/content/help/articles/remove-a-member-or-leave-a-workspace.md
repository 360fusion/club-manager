Owners and admins can remove people from a team, and anyone who is not the owner can leave one.

## Remove a member

1. Open the team and go to **Team**.
2. Find the person in the table.
3. Press the option to remove them and confirm.

They lose access to the team straight away. An admin cannot remove another admin or the owner. See [What each role can do](/help/what-each-role-can-do).

## Leave a team

1. Open the team you want to leave.
2. Go to **Team**.
3. Press **Leave this team** and confirm.

You are no longer a member and the team disappears from your list at [Teams](/workspaces). To get back in, someone has to [invite you](/help/invite-a-teammate) again.

## If you are the owner

The owner does not have a **Leave this team** button. You have two choices:

- Hand the team to someone else first. See [Transfer or delete a team](/help/transfer-or-delete-a-workspace). Then you can leave.
- Remove the other members, then delete the team.

## If something goes wrong

- If you cannot remove someone, check your role. Only owners and admins can remove people.
- If a person has an invitation waiting, it is listed on the **Team** page. Withdraw it there.
- You cannot leave your own **Personal** team.
- If you picked the wrong person, invite them again.
