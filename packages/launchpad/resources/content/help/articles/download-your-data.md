You can download a copy of your data as a file from the Data and privacy page.

1. Go to **Account** then **Data and privacy**, or open [Data and privacy](/account/privacy).
2. Press **Download my data**.
3. Save the file when your browser offers it.

The file is yours to keep. Store it somewhere safe, as it may contain personal information.

## Good times to do this

- Before you [close your account](/help/close-your-account).
- Before you [delete a team](/help/transfer-or-delete-a-workspace).
- Whenever you want your own backup.

## If something goes wrong

- If nothing downloads, check that your browser is not blocking the download, then try again.
- If the file is large, give it a little time to finish before you open it.
- If you cannot find the file, look in your browser's downloads list.
- If you need help reading it, or you want a copy in another form, tell us what you need.
