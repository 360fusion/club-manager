If the site has them switched on, you can sign up or sign in with your Google or Microsoft account instead of a password.

1. Go to [Sign in](/login) or [Register](/register).
2. Press **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.
3. Choose your account on the Google or Microsoft page and agree to share your name and email address with {{product}}.
4. You come back to {{product}}, signed in.

If you are new, an account is made for you on the way, with a **Personal** team. If you already have an account with the same email address, you are signed in to it.

## Setting a password later

An account made this way may never have had a password. You can sign in with the button every time. If you would like a password as well, you can add one on **Account** then **Security**. See [Change your email or password](/help/change-your-email-or-password).

## If something goes wrong

- If you do not see the buttons, this site has not turned them on. Sign in with your email and password instead, or [create an account](/help/create-an-account).
- If you pick a different Google or Microsoft account than last time, you may land in a new, empty account. Sign out and choose the right one.
- If you have two-factor sign-in on, you may still be asked for a code. See [Turn on two-factor sign-in](/help/turn-on-two-factor-sign-in).
- If the Google or Microsoft page shows an error, close it and try again.
