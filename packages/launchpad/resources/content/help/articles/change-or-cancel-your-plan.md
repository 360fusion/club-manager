You change or cancel a plan from the Billing page of the team. Owners and admins can do both.

## Change your plan

1. Open the team and go to **Billing**. See [See your plan](/help/see-your-plan).
2. Press **Change plan**. The other plans are listed.
3. Choose the one you want.
4. Read the review page. It says when the change starts.
5. Confirm.

When the change starts depends on what you pick:

- A step up to a bigger plan, or yearly instead of monthly, starts now. Stripe charges only the difference for the time left in your period.
- A step down, or monthly instead of yearly, starts at your next renewal. You keep what you have until then.

## Cancel your plan

1. Go to **Billing**.
2. Press **Cancel plan**. Stripe's cancel page opens.
3. Follow the steps there.

Your plan runs to the end of the period you have paid for. After that, the team moves to the free plan. Nothing is deleted.

## If something goes wrong

- If you cannot see **Change plan** or **Cancel plan**, you may not be an owner or admin. See [What each role can do](/help/what-each-role-can-do).
- If you cancelled by mistake, go back to **Billing** before the period ends and choose a plan again.
- If the review page shows a start date you did not expect, go back without confirming.
- To compare plans, see [Pricing](/pricing).
