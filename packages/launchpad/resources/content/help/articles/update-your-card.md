You update your card in Stripe's secure portal, which you open from the Billing page. Owners and admins can do this.

1. Open the team and go to **Billing**.
2. Press **Card, address and VAT number**. Stripe's secure portal opens.
3. Add your new card, or change the details you want to change.
4. Save, then return to {{product}}.

Your card details are handled by Stripe. We never see your full card number.

Each team has its own plan and its own card. If you look after more than one team, repeat the steps in each one.

## When to do this

- Your card has expired or been replaced.
- A payment failed. See [What happens if a payment fails](/help/what-happens-if-a-payment-fails).
- You want invoices to carry a different address or a VAT number. See [Find an invoice and add a VAT number](/help/find-an-invoice-and-add-a-vat-number).

## If something goes wrong

- If you cannot see the button, you may not be an owner or admin of the team.
- If the portal does not open, check that your browser is not blocking pop-ups, then try again.
- If your bank declines the new card, ask your bank or try another card.
- If the page still shows the old card, refresh it after a minute.
