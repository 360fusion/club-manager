Only the owner of a team can hand it to someone else or delete it. We ask for your password both times.

## Transfer ownership

1. Open the team and go to its settings.
2. Find **Transfer ownership**.
3. Pick another member of the team.
4. Type your password to confirm.

The person you pick becomes the owner. The new owner needs to be a member already, so [invite them](/help/invite-a-teammate) first if they are not. See [What each role can do](/help/what-each-role-can-do).

## Delete a team

1. Open the team and go to its settings.
2. Find **Delete team**.
3. Type your password to confirm.

A team you delete is gone, so [download your data](/help/download-your-data) first if you want a copy. You cannot delete your **Personal** team.

## Other settings

The same settings page lets you change the team name and time zone.

## If something goes wrong

- If you do not see the options, you are not the owner. Ask the owner to do it.
- If you want to leave a team you own, hand it over first. See [Remove a member or leave a team](/help/remove-a-member-or-leave-a-workspace).
- If the team has a paid plan, [cancel the plan](/help/change-or-cancel-your-plan) before you delete it.
- If your password is refused, check it and try again.
