You create your {{product}} account on the [Register](/register) page with your name, your email address and a password.

1. Go to [Register](/register).
2. Type your name and your email address.
3. Choose a password and type it again in the second box to confirm it.
4. Tick the box to accept the Terms.
5. Press the button to create your account.

We then show a **Check your inbox** page. Open the email we sent you and use the link in it to confirm your address. [Read how to verify your email](/help/verify-your-email).

Everyone gets a **Personal** team when they sign up. You can [create a business team](/help/invite-a-teammate) later and invite people to it.

If the site offers **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**, you can use one of those buttons instead of a password. See [Sign in with Google or Microsoft](/help/sign-in-with-google-or-microsoft).

If you want to buy before you sign up, you can. Pick a plan on the [Pricing](/pricing) page. You create your account on the way, then pay.

## If something goes wrong

- If the form says the email address is already in use, go to [Sign in](/login) instead. If you cannot remember the password, use **Forgot your password?**.
- If you cannot press the button, check that the Terms box is ticked and that both passwords match.
- If the email does not arrive, look in your spam folder, then press **Send the link again** on the **Check your inbox** page.
