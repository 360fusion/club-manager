You can change your password yourself on the Security page. Changing your email address is not self-service, so you need to contact us.

## Change your password

1. Go to **Account** then **Security**, or open [Security](/account/security).
2. Find the password section.
3. Type your current password, then your new password, and confirm it.
4. Save the change.

If you made your account with Google or Microsoft and never had a password, we do not ask for a current one. You can just choose a new password.

If you cannot remember your current password, [reset it by email](/help/reset-your-password) instead.

## Change your email address

You cannot change the email address on your account in the app. [Contact us](/contact) from the address you use now, tell us the new address you want, and we will help you with it.

Your email address matters for invitations too. An invitation only works for the exact address it was sent to. See [Invite a teammate](/help/invite-a-teammate).

## If something goes wrong

- If the site says your current password is wrong, check caps lock, or use the reset email.
- If your new password is refused, choose a longer one that you do not use elsewhere.
- If you have two-factor sign-in on, it stays on after a password change. See [Turn on two-factor sign-in](/help/turn-on-two-factor-sign-in).
