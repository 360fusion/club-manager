Your invoices are listed on the Billing page, and your address and VAT number are kept in Stripe's secure portal.

## Find an invoice

1. Open the team and go to **Billing**. Owners and admins can open it.
2. Look at the list of invoices.
3. Open the one you need.

Invoices belong to the team that paid them. If you have several teams, check the right one.

## Add or change a VAT number

1. Go to **Billing**.
2. Press **Card, address and VAT number**. Stripe's secure portal opens.
3. Enter your billing address and your VAT number.
4. Save, then return to {{product}}.

## Does an old invoice change?

A new address or VAT number is used on invoices from then on. Invoices already issued stay as they were. If you need an old one corrected, [contact us](/contact) and say which invoice.

## If something goes wrong

- If the invoice list is empty, the team may be on the free plan, or its first payment has not been taken yet. See [See your plan](/help/see-your-plan).
- If you cannot see the **Billing** page, you may not be an owner or admin. See [What each role can do](/help/what-each-role-can-do).
- If the portal rejects your VAT number, check it for typing mistakes and include any country prefix.
- Your card is changed in the same portal. See [Update your card](/help/update-your-card).
