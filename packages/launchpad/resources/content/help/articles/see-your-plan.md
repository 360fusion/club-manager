The Billing page of a team shows what you pay for, when it renews and how you pay. Owners and admins can open it.

1. Open the team.
2. Go to **Billing**.

On the page you will see:

- Your plan and your billing period (monthly or yearly).
- The number of seats, if your plan is charged per seat.
- The date your plan renews.
- The card on file.
- Your invoices.

Plans are charged per team, so each team has its own Billing page. To compare plans, see [Pricing](/pricing), which has a monthly and yearly switch.

## What you can do from the page

- **Card, address and VAT number** opens Stripe's secure portal. See [Update your card](/help/update-your-card).
- **Change plan** lists the other plans. See [Change or cancel your plan](/help/change-or-cancel-your-plan).
- **Cancel plan** opens Stripe's cancel page.
- Your invoices are listed on the page. See [Find an invoice](/help/find-an-invoice-and-add-a-vat-number).

## If something goes wrong

- If you cannot find the Billing page, you may not be an owner or admin of that team. See [What each role can do](/help/what-each-role-can-do).
- If you bought without an account first, you will find the plan under the team created for you on the way.
- If a notice at the top says a payment failed, see [What happens if a payment fails](/help/what-happens-if-a-payment-fails).
