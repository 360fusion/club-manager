You close your account yourself from the Data and privacy page. It stops straight away.

Before you start, [download your data](/help/download-your-data) if you want a copy.

1. Go to **Account** then **Data and privacy**, or open [Data and privacy](/account/privacy).
2. Find **Close your account**.
3. Type **CLOSE** in the box.
4. Type your password to confirm.
5. Press the button to close the account.

## What happens next

- Your account stops working at once and you are signed out.
- Anything it pays for is ended.
- We keep your account for {{retention_days}} days. In that time it can be restored if you [contact us](/contact).
- After that, it is removed for good and cannot be brought back.

## If you own a team with other people in it

You cannot close the account while you own a team that has other members. First do one of these:

- Hand the team over. See [Transfer or delete a team](/help/transfer-or-delete-a-workspace).
- Remove the other members. See [Remove a member or leave a team](/help/remove-a-member-or-leave-a-workspace).

## If something goes wrong

- If the button stays grey, check that you typed CLOSE in capitals.
- If your password is refused, [reset it](/help/reset-your-password) first.
- If you closed it by mistake, contact us within {{retention_days}} days.
