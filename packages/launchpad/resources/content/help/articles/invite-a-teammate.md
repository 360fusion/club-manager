Owners and admins invite people from the Team page of a team, by email address.

1. Open the team you want to add someone to. To make a new business team first, go to [Teams](/workspaces) and press **Create a team**.
2. Open the **Team** page.
3. Type the person's email address.
4. Choose a role in the role chooser. See [What each role can do](/help/what-each-role-can-do).
5. Press the button to send the invitation.

You need a confirmed email address of your own to invite people. See [Confirm your email address](/help/verify-your-email).

## What the other person does

They get an email with an **Accept the invitation** button. It works for 7 days. They must sign in, or sign up, with the very address you invited, and confirm that address. Then they join the team with the role you chose.

## Withdraw an invitation

Invitations that have not been accepted are listed on the **Team** page. Press the option to withdraw one and it stops working.

## If something goes wrong

- If the person signs in with a different address, the invitation does nothing. Invite the address they actually use, or ask them to use the invited one.
- If 7 days have passed, withdraw the old invitation and send a new one.
- If the email did not arrive, ask them to check spam, then send it again.
- If you cannot see the invite box, you may not be an owner or admin of that team.
