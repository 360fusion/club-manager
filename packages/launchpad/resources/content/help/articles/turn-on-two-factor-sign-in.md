Two-factor sign-in asks for a code from your phone as well as your password, so a stolen password is not enough to get into your account.

1. Go to **Account** then **Security**, or open [Security](/account/security).
2. Press **Set up**.
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app on your phone. If you cannot scan it, type the key shown beside it into the app.
4. Type the code the app shows into the box.
5. Press **Turn on**.

We then show 8 recovery codes. They are shown once, so copy them somewhere safe now, such as a password manager.

## Signing in afterwards

After your password, type the code from your app. If you have lost your phone, press **Lost your phone? Use a recovery code** and type one of your recovery codes.

## Managing it

- **Make new recovery codes** gives you a fresh set. The old ones stop working.
- **Turn off** removes the second step. We ask for your password first.

## If something goes wrong

- If the code is refused, check the clock on your phone is set automatically.
- Five wrong codes in a row lock the account out for five minutes. Wait, then try again.
- If you have no phone and no recovery codes, [contact us](/contact). We will need to check it is really you.
- Forgotten your password as well? See [Reset your password](/help/reset-your-password).
