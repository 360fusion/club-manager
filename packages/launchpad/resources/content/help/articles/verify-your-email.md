You confirm your email address by opening the link in the email we send you when you sign up.

1. Open your inbox and find the email from {{product}}.
2. Press the link in the email.
3. You are taken back to the site and your address is confirmed.

Depending on how the site is set up, a new account either has to confirm its address before it can use the app, or can start straight away. In the second case you still need a confirmed address to invite people, post or buy.

## If the email does not arrive

1. Sign in at [Sign in](/login). The **Check your inbox** page appears if your address is not yet confirmed.
2. Press **Send the link again**.
3. Wait a minute or two, then check your inbox and your spam folder.

## If something goes wrong

- Make sure you typed your email address correctly when you [created your account](/help/create-an-account). If you did not, changing it is not something you can do yourself: [contact us](/contact).
- Use the newest email. An older link may no longer work.
- If your mail provider blocks the message, add {{support_email}} to your contacts and ask for the link again.
- If the link opens but you are asked to sign in, sign in with the same address you registered with.
