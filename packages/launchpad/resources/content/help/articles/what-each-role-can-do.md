Every person in a team has one of four roles: Owner, Admin, Manager or Member.

## The roles

- **Owner**: can do everything. Only the owner can transfer ownership and delete the team.
- **Admin**: manages members, team settings and billing.
- **Manager**: can see the member list.
- **Member**: has basic use of the team.

Each team has one owner. Your role is shown against your name in the table on the **Team** page.

## Who can change what

1. Open the team and go to **Team**.
2. Owners and admins can change a person's role, remove people, invite by email and withdraw waiting invitations.
3. An admin cannot change another admin or the owner.
4. Only the owner can [transfer ownership or delete the team](/help/transfer-or-delete-a-workspace).

Billing pages are open to owners and admins only. See [See your plan](/help/see-your-plan).

## Change someone's role

1. Go to **Team**.
2. Find the person in the table.
3. Pick the new role.
4. Save the change.

## If something goes wrong

- If you cannot change someone's role, check your own role. Admins cannot change other admins or the owner.
- If you cannot see the **Team** page contents you expect, ask an owner or admin to check your role.
- To make someone the owner, the current owner has to transfer ownership to them.
- To add someone new, see [Invite a teammate](/help/invite-a-teammate).
