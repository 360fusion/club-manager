It depends on the circumstances, and our [Refund policy](/refunds) sets out exactly when we refund. If you were charged by mistake or something is wrong with your bill, [Contact us](/contact) as soon as you can and tell us the email on your account. We will look into it and reply with what we can do. You can also cancel at any time so that you are not charged again.
