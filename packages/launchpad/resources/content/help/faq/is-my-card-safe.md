---
question: "Is my card safe?"
keywords: [card, payment, security, stripe, pci]
category: billing
updated: 2026-11-02
---
Yes. Payments are handled by Stripe, a specialist payment provider. Your card number goes straight to Stripe and never reaches our servers, so we cannot see or store it. We only keep what we need to show your plan and invoices. To change the card on your account, follow [Update your card](/help/update-your-card). Stripe is listed on our [Subprocessors](/subprocessors) page.
