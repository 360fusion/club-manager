---
question: "How do I delete my account?"
keywords: [delete, close, remove, account, erase]
category: accounts
updated: 2026-11-02
---
Yes, you can do it by closing your account from Data and privacy in your account. Follow [Close your account](/help/close-your-account) for the steps. If you want a copy of your information first, see [Download your data](/help/download-your-data). Closing stops the account at once and ends what it pays for, so you are not billed again. We keep it for {{retention_days}} days in case you change your mind, then it is removed for good, except anything we must keep by law.
