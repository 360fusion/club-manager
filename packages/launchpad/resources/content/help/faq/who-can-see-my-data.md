Only you, the people you add to your team, and the limited set of people who need access to keep the service running. What your teammates can see depends on their role: see [What each role can do](/help/what-each-role-can-do). We do not sell your data. The companies that help us run {{product}} are listed on [Subprocessors](/subprocessors), and the details are in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
