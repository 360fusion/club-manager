---
question: "Can I change my plan?"
keywords: [change, upgrade, downgrade, cancel, plan]
category: billing
updated: 2026-11-02
---
Yes, you can do it by opening your account's billing page and choosing a different plan, or cancelling. You do not need to contact anyone. A step up starts at once and Stripe charges only the difference for the time left; a step down starts at your next renewal, so you keep what you have paid for. The full steps are in [How do I change or cancel my plan?](/help/change-or-cancel-your-plan). Prices for every plan are on the [Pricing](/pricing) page.
