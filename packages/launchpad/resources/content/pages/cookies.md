Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. This page explains the cookies {{product}} uses at {{site_url}}. In short: a few essential ones are always on, and everything else stays off until you say yes.

## 1. Strictly necessary cookies

These make the service work. We set them without asking, because the service cannot work properly without them. They do not track you around the web.

- **Session cookie:** keeps you signed in while you use the service.
- **CSRF cookie:** protects forms and actions from being sent by someone else on your behalf.
- **Remember-me cookie:** keeps you signed in on this device for longer, but only if you tick that option when you sign in.
- **Cookie consent choice:** remembers what you decided about the optional cookies below, so we do not ask you again on every visit.

## 2. Optional cookies

Analytics and marketing cookies are optional. We set them only after you agree, and never before.

- **Analytics cookies** help us understand how the service is used, for example which pages are visited, so we can make it better.
- **Marketing cookies** help measure whether our own promotion works, or show you relevant messages elsewhere.

Whether these are in use depends on how the service is set up. If none are active, there is nothing to switch on or off.

## 4. Your choices

When you first visit, a banner asks for your choice. You can accept all optional cookies, or reject them. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and the service works the same either way.

You can change your mind at any time. Use the cookie settings link in the page footer to open your choices again and update them. If you withdraw consent, we stop setting those cookies, and you can delete any already saved.

You can also delete cookies, or block them, in your browser settings. Blocking the strictly necessary ones means you may not be able to sign in or use the forms.

## 5. Third parties

Some optional cookies come from other companies, such as an analytics provider. They have their own rules for how they use the data. The companies that help us run the service are listed on our [Subprocessors](/subprocessors) page.
