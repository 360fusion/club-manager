This policy explains how {{company}} handles your personal data when you use {{product}} at {{site_url}}. In short: we collect what we need to run your account and the service, we do not sell it, and you can ask us about it at any time. We handle personal data in line with the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR where they apply.

Effective from {{effective_date}}.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

{{company}}, {{address}}, decides why and how your personal data is used. That makes us the "controller". For questions about privacy, write to {{dpo_email}}.

There is one exception. When a business uses {{product}} and puts personal data of its own customers or staff into its team, that business is the controller and we are its "processor". Our [Data Processing Addendum](/dpa) covers that case.

## 2. What we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account details:** your name, email address and password. We store the password in a scrambled form (hashed), never as plain text.
- **Sign-in details:** if you use Google or Microsoft to sign in, we receive your name and email address from them. If you turn on two-factor sign-in, we keep the information needed to check your authenticator app codes.
- **Team details:** team names, the people you invite, and the role each person holds (owner, admin, manager or member).
- **Content you add:** whatever you put into the service.
- **Messages:** what you send through the contact form or by email.

### Billing information

Paid plans are billed by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and we never see or store them. We keep a record of your plan, billing period, invoices and the status of your payments, and the billing country and tax details needed for VAT.

### Information collected automatically

- **Technical data:** your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages or actions used. We use it to keep the service secure and working.
- **API activity:** for a team's API tokens we keep how many calls each token made and how many failed, for each day, for 90 days, and the IP address and program name that last used it, for as long as the token exists. Team owners and admins can see this for their team.
- **Error reports:** when something breaks, a report may include technical details about what you were doing.
- **Cookies:** see section 8 and our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## 3. Why we use your data, and our legal reasons

| What we do | Why | Legal reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create and run your account and teams | To give you the service | Contract |
| Take payment, send invoices, work out tax | To bill you correctly | Contract and legal duty |
| Send emails the service needs (sign-in, invitations, receipts, security notices) | To keep the service working | Contract and legitimate interests |
| Answer your questions | To give support | Legitimate interests |
| Keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix errors | To protect you and us | Legitimate interests |

"Legitimate interests" means we have a good reason to use the data that does not override your rights. You can ask us how we weighed that up.

## 5. Where your data goes

Some of our providers may handle data outside the UK or the European Economic Area. When that happens, we make sure an appropriate safeguard is in place, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses approved by the regulator. You can ask us for more detail at {{dpo_email}}.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Your account and content:** for as long as your account is open.
- **When you close your account:** access stops at once. We then keep the account for {{retention_days}} days so it can be restored, and after that it is removed for good.
- **Billing records:** we keep invoices and payment records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after the account is gone.
- **Messages to support:** for as long as needed to help you and to keep a record of what was agreed.
- **Backups and logs:** these drop out on their normal cycle after the data has been removed.

You can download a copy of your data from your account before you close it.

## 7. Security

We protect your data with measures suited to the risk. These include encryption in transit, hashed passwords, optional two-factor sign-in, limited and logged staff access, and backups. You can read more on our [Security](/security) page. No system is perfectly safe, and if there is a breach that puts you at risk, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

## 8. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, protect forms from forgery, remember your choices and, if you ask, keep you logged in. We use analytics and marketing cookies only if you agree. You can change your mind at any time. The full list is on our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
- correct anything that is wrong;
- delete your data (closing your account does this after {{retention_days}} days);
- limit how we use your data, or object to uses based on legitimate interests;
- give you your data in a form you can move to another service;
- stop using your data for any purpose you gave consent for, by withdrawing consent.

Much of this you can do yourself in your account. For anything else, write to {{dpo_email}}. We will reply within one month. We may need to check who you are first.

If you are not happy with our answer, you have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

## 10. Children

{{product}} is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, tell us at {{dpo_email}} and we will delete it.
