Your account and your data matter. This page explains in plain words what we do to protect them, what you can do too, and how to tell us if you find a problem. We do not claim certifications, and we do not promise that nothing can ever go wrong.

## What we do

- **Encryption in transit.** Traffic between your browser and {{product}} is encrypted, so others on the network cannot read it.
- **Passwords.** We never store your password as plain text. It is hashed, which means we cannot read it ourselves.
- **Two-factor sign-in.** You can turn on a second step using an authenticator app. We recommend it, especially for owners and admins.
- **Card details.** Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and we never see or store them.
- **Limited staff access.** Only the people who need access to do their job can get to systems with customer data, and that access is logged.
- **Roles in teams.** Owners, admins, managers and members have different permissions, so people only get the access they need.
- **Backups.** We keep backups so data can be restored if something goes wrong.
- **Service providers.** We use providers for hosting, email delivery, error monitoring and payments, and we have contracts that require them to protect data. See our [Subprocessors](/subprocessors) page.

## What you can do

- Use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else.
- Turn on two-factor sign-in in your account.
- Give people the lowest role they need in a team, and remove members who no longer need access.
- Watch for emails that pretend to be from us. We will never ask for your password by email.
- Tell us quickly if you think your account has been used by someone else.

## Report a vulnerability

If you think you have found a security problem in {{product}}, please tell us. We would much rather hear about it than not.

Email {{support_email}} with the subject "Security report". Please include:

- what you found and where;
- the steps to repeat it, as clearly as you can;
- what you think the effect could be;
- how we can contact you for questions.

Please do not include real customer data in your report.

### What we will do

We aim to acknowledge your report promptly, look into it, and keep you updated. We will fix real problems as quickly as we reasonably can, and we are glad to say thank you.

### What we ask of you

- Do not access, change or delete data that is not yours.
- Do not disrupt the service, for example with heavy automated traffic.
- Do not tell others about the problem until we have had a reasonable chance to fix it.
- Do not use the problem for anything other than reporting it.

### Safe harbour

If you act in good faith and follow these rules, we will not take legal action against you for your research, and we will not ask the authorities to. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask us first.

## Personal data breaches

If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires. More is in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
