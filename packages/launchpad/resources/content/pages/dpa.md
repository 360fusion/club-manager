This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") applies when you use {{product}} for a business or organisation and put personal data into it. In short: you decide what happens to that data, and {{company}} handles it only on your instructions and with proper care. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms) and applies from {{effective_date}}.

## 1. Who does what

For personal data that you or your team members put into {{product}}, the customer is the "controller" and {{company}} is the "processor". This means you decide why and how the data is used, and we process it for you.

For personal data we use for our own purposes, such as running your account and billing, we are a controller. That is covered by our [Privacy Policy](/privacy), not this DPA.

We handle personal data in line with the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR where they apply. Words such as "personal data", "processing", "controller" and "processor" have the meaning they have in those laws.

## 2. What we process and why

- **Subject matter:** providing the {{product}} service.
- **Duration:** as long as your account is open, plus the {{retention_days}} days described in section 9.
- **Nature and purpose:** storing, organising, showing and transferring the data so that you and your team members can use the service.
- **Types of data:** the content you add, and account details of your members such as names, email addresses and roles.
- **People concerned:** your team members and anyone whose data you choose to add.

## 3. Our promises as processor

We will:

- process personal data only on your written instructions, which are these terms, your use of the service and the settings you choose. If we think an instruction breaks the law, we will tell you;
- make sure everyone who works with the data is bound by confidentiality;
- keep the data secure, as set out in section 4;
- not sell the data, or use it for our own marketing;
- tell you if we receive a request about your data from someone else or from an authority, unless the law stops us, and not answer it without your say-so.

## 4. Security

We use technical and organisational measures that suit the risk. These include encryption in transit, hashed passwords, optional two-factor sign-in, limited and logged staff access, and backups. Card details are handled by Stripe and never stored by us. More is on our [Security](/security) page. We review these measures as the service changes.

We have a contract with each subprocessor that asks for protection equal to what is in this DPA. We remain responsible for them. If we plan to add or replace one, we will tell customers by email beforehand. Anyone who wants to be told can write to {{support_email}}. If you object on reasonable data protection grounds, tell us within a reasonable time and we will work with you to find a solution. If we cannot, you may close your account.

## 6. Helping you meet your duties

Taking the nature of the processing into account, we will help you:

- answer requests from people who want to use their rights, for example access or deletion. Many of these can be done within the service;
- carry out data protection impact assessments and talk to regulators, where it relates to our service;
- keep to your security and breach duties.

If this takes unusual effort, we may ask for a reasonable fee, and tell you before we do.

## 7. Personal data breaches

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your data, we will tell you without undue delay. We will give you what we know: what happened, the data involved, the likely effects, and what we are doing about it. We will keep you updated as we learn more.

## 8. International transfers

Some of our subprocessors may process data outside the UK or the European Economic Area. Where they do, we make sure an appropriate safeguard is in place, such as an adequacy decision or approved standard contractual clauses. You agree to these transfers on that basis.

## 9. Closing your account and deleting data

When you close your account, access stops at once. We keep the account for {{retention_days}} days so it can be restored, and then remove it for good, including copies we hold as a processor. You can download a copy of your data from your account before that. Backups drop out on their normal cycle. We may keep data longer only if the law requires, and then only for that reason.

## 10. Checks and audits

We will give you the information you reasonably need to show we are keeping to this DPA. If that is not enough, you may ask for an audit on reasonable notice, no more than once a year unless there has been a breach or a regulator requires it. The audit must not disrupt our service or expose other customers' data, and you cover its costs.

## 11. Liability and the rest

The limits on responsibility in the Terms of Service apply to this DPA. If this DPA and the Terms disagree about personal data, this DPA comes first. The law of {{jurisdiction}} governs this DPA.
