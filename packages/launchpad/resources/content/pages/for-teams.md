# {{product}} for teams

{{product}} works as well for a group as it does for one person. You set up a team, invite the people you work with, and decide what each of them can do.

## Teams

A team is the shared place where your team works. Everything in it belongs to the team, not to one person's login, so work carries on when people come and go.

## Roles

Every person in a team has one of four roles: owner, admin, manager or member. Owners and admins look after the team and its settings. Managers help run day-to-day work, and members get on with their own tasks. For the detail, see [What each role can do](/help/what-each-role-can-do).

## Invitations

To add someone, send them an invitation by email. They follow the link, check the details on the Join page and accept. Until they accept, nothing changes in your team, and you can withdraw an invitation you no longer want.

## Billing

The team plan is billed per seat through Stripe, so you pay for the people you add. You can change the number of seats yourself as your team grows or shrinks. Payments are handled securely by Stripe, and VAT is taken care of for you.

See the [Pricing](/pricing) page for current plans and prices.
