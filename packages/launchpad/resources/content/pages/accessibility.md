{{company}} wants {{product}} at {{site_url}} to be usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technology or who cannot use a mouse. This page says what we aim for, what we do, and where we fall short. It is honest, not a boast.

## Our aim

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We do not claim that the whole service meets that standard yet. Accessibility is something we keep working on, not something we finished.

## What we do

- **Keyboard use:** we aim for everything to work without a mouse, with a visible focus marker so you can see where you are.
- **Labels:** form fields have labels, and error messages say what went wrong and how to fix it.
- **Structure:** pages use headings and landmarks in a sensible order, so screen readers can move around.
- **Contrast and text:** we choose colours with enough contrast, and text can be enlarged in the browser without things breaking.
- **Reduced motion:** where we use movement, we respect your device setting to reduce it.
- **Plain language:** we write short sentences and avoid jargon.

## Known limits

We know there are likely to be gaps. Some parts of the service, especially newer or less used screens, may not yet work fully with every screen reader or with keyboard use alone. Some content that you or other people add may not be accessible, because we do not control it. Some third-party parts, such as the Stripe payment pages, follow their own accessibility practices.

If you find a barrier, we want to hear about it.

## Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, or you need information in a different form, please [contact us](/contact) or email {{support_email}}. It helps if you tell us:

- what page or action you were trying to use;
- what went wrong;
- the browser and any assistive technology you were using.

We will aim to reply promptly and, where we can, fix the problem or find another way to help you. If you are not happy with our reply, you can take the matter to the body that enforces accessibility rules where you live.
