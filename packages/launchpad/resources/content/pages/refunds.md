This page explains how cancelling and refunds work for paid plans on {{product}}. In short: you can cancel at any time, your plan then runs to the end of what you have paid for, and refunds are limited as set out below. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

## 1. How to cancel

You manage your plan through Stripe's customer portal, which you can open from your account. There you can cancel, change plan, update your card and download invoices. You do not need to contact us.

When you cancel, nothing is taken after the end of the period you have already paid for. You keep your paid features until then, and the team moves to the free plan afterwards.

## 2. Monthly plans

You can cancel at any time. Your plan runs to the end of the current month and then stops renewing. We do not refund part-months, so unused days are not returned.

## 3. Yearly plans

You can cancel at any time. Your plan runs to the end of the year you have paid for and then stops renewing. After the first 14 days, we do not give a pro-rata refund for unused months, unless the law says we must.

## 4. The 14-day cooling-off period for consumers

If you are a consumer, meaning you are buying for personal use and not for a business, the law may give you the right to change your mind within 14 days of buying a paid plan and get your money back.

If you started using the paid service during those 14 days, we may deduct a fair amount for the time used, as the law allows.

To use this right, email {{support_email}} or use the [contact form](/contact) within the 14 days and say you want to cancel. We will refund you to the original payment method within 14 days of hearing from you.

## 5. Upgrades and downgrades

An upgrade starts straight away, and you pay the difference for the rest of the current period (proration). A downgrade starts at your next renewal, so you keep what you paid for until then. Moving to a lower plan does not earn a refund of the difference.

## 6. Where we got it wrong

If we charge you by mistake, charge twice, or do not deliver the service as described, tell us at {{support_email}} and we will put it right, including a refund where that is fair. Nothing here takes away rights you have by law.

## 7. Closing your account

Closing your account ends what it pays for at once, and does not itself trigger a refund. If you want a plan to run to the end of the paid period, cancel the plan first and close the account afterwards. Refunds go back through Stripe to the original payment method, and normally appear within a few days. See the [Help centre](/help) for more.
