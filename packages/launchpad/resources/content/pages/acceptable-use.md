This policy sets out what you can and cannot do with {{product}}. In short: be decent, keep it legal, do not harm the service or other people, and look after your account. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms) and applies to everyone in a team, including owners, admins, managers and members.

## 1. The basics

You may use {{product}} for lawful purposes and in line with the plan you are on. You are responsible for what you and the people you invite do in your team. Team owners should make sure their members know these rules.

## 2. What is not allowed

### Illegal or harmful content

Do not upload, store or share anything that:

- is against the law where you or we operate;
- infringes someone else's copyright, trade mark or other rights;
- is defamatory, threatening, harassing or hateful;
- exploits or endangers children;
- contains malware or code meant to cause harm.

### Misuse of the service

Do not:

- try to get into accounts, teams or data that are not yours;
- test, scan or probe the service for weaknesses without our permission (if you find a problem, please follow the steps on our [Security](/security) page);
- overload the service, or use bots or scripts to send large volumes of requests;
- get round limits, plan restrictions or payment;
- resell or sublicence access in a way your plan does not allow;
- reverse-engineer or copy the service, except where the law gives you that right.

### Misuse of email and messages

Do not use invitations, the contact form or any other feature to send spam, phishing or unwanted messages. Only invite people you have a reason to invite.

### Pretending to be someone else

Do not pretend to be another person or organisation, and do not use someone else's account without their permission.

## 3. Keeping your account safe

Use a strong, unique password, and turn on two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app. Do not share your sign-in details. If you think your account has been used by someone else, tell us at {{support_email}} straight away.

## 4. What we may do

If we think this policy has been broken, we may:

- ask you to remove the content or stop the behaviour;
- limit or suspend features, a member or a team;
- close an account, with or without notice where the breach is serious or the law requires it;
- tell the authorities when we are required to, or when someone may be at risk.

Where we reasonably can, we will tell you what the problem is and give you the chance to put it right. If we close your account, the usual rules about retention and downloading your data apply, as described in the Terms.

## 5. Reporting a problem

If you see something that breaks this policy, or you think something has been misused, [contact us](/contact) or email {{support_email}}. Please include where you saw it and what happened. For security issues, use the steps on the [Security](/security) page.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. If a change matters, we will email you or show a notice in the service.
